Did buses push riders onto the Yellow Line?

In its first eight days the Yellow Line drew no riders away from bus-linked metro stations (+0.65% per SD, p = 0.64). Frequency did matter: cutting the wait from 25 to 19 minutes added 8.7% riders. Explore the stations, rerun the test under any specification, and try the headway calculator.

    How it was built

    One HDFS pipeline, 13 Spark notebooks on Kaggle

    Ingest26 bus GTFS snapshots, 12 M metro trips into HDFS as Parquet
    ScoreBus-link score per station on the OSM walking network
    FreezeTop-10 list hashed before ridership was opened
    TestOD fixed effects, bootstrap, randomisation inference
    LearnSpark MLlib with a shuffled-bus placebo
    ScaleSpark 4 cores: 2.2x faster than pandas on 38.6 M rows
